# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Assume good intent, discuss the
work rather than the person, and make room for people with different levels of
experience. Harassment, discrimination, threats, and publication of another
person's private information are not acceptable.

## Scope

These standards apply to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, code
review, and other project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository's security or
maintainer contact features. Maintainers may edit or remove content and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.
